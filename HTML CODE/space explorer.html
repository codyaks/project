Space exploration is the use of space technology and astronomy to study the universe, and includes both robotic and human spaceflight. Some reasons for space exploration include: Curiosity The same curiosity that led people to explore the Earth's oceans, mountains, and poles also drives space exploration. Practical value Space exploration has many practical applications, such as weather forecasting, navigation, scientific research, and telecommunications. Learning about the world Space exploration allows us to learn about the world around us, just like exploring the Earth's forests, mountains, or sea.
