Religious festivals:

these include Diwali, Holi, Rakhi, Navaratri, Guru Purnima, Shivratri, Ganesh Chaturthi, and more

. Harvest festivals: These include Sankranti, Onam, and Pongal. Other festivals: These include:

Types of festivals

Eid-ul-Fitr: An important Islamic festival that marks the end of Ramadan. Muslims celebrate by attending prayers, decorating their homes, and exchanging gifts. Gujarat Kite Festival: A festival that takes place in January to celebrate the start of summer and the harvest. Ganesh Chaturthi: A 10-day festival that celebrates the god of wisdom, prosperity, and good fortune. International Film Festival of India: A festival that takes place in Goa. Aravali International Film Festival: A film festival that takes place in Delhi. Lollapalooza India: A music festival that takes place in Mumbai. New Delhi World Book Fair: A literature festival that takes place in New Delhi. Mahindra Sanatkada Lucknow Festival: An arts and crafts festival that takes place in Lucknow. Kala Ghoda Arts Festival: An arts festival that takes place in Mumbai.
