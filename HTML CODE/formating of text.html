About us

Adarsh Bal Niketan provides all round education to develop a sound mind, a robust physique and strong moral values. As an educational institution located within the campus of The Indian Institute of Technology (formerly, University of Roorkee), it strives for excellence while upholding the highest secular values enshrined in our Constitution. Over the last sixty-four years, ABN has striven to produce citizens who will serve the best interests of the Nation in various walks of life and society.
